ABOUT

From the first sketch
to the finished set.

10+ years of experience across art direction, set design, props and visual development.

My work moves between the conceptual and the physical — from visual development, painting and environment design to sets, miniatures, props and practical production. I enjoy working across materials, techniques and disciplines, finding the right way to turn an idea into something tangible and visually distinctive.

With a background in Fine Arts and experience across film, animation, commercial and independent productions, I can move from the first sketch and visual research through fabrication, finishing and on-set execution. I am particularly interested in projects where the visual world is not simply decoration, but an active part of the storytelling.
